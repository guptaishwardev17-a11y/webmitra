Pricing & Plans

What's included in the Basic ₹4,999 plan?

+

Up to 5 pages, mobile-responsive design, contact form, basic SEO, Google Maps, WhatsApp button, 1 month support, 5–7 day delivery. Perfect for small businesses needing a clean online presence.

What extra does Pro ₹9,999 offer?

+

Everything in Basic plus: up to 10 pages, premium UI/UX, custom animations, full SEO, Google Analytics, blog section, speed optimization, and 3 months free support.

Are there hidden charges?

+

Never. Quote = final price. Domain (~₹700–1000/yr) and hosting (~₹2000–5000/yr) are third-party costs paid directly to the provider — we help you set these up.

Can I pay in installments?

+

Yes, for larger projects we offer 50% advance and 50% on delivery. WhatsApp us to discuss.

Process & Delivery

How long does it take?

+

Basic: 5–7 days. Pro: 7–10 days. Custom: agreed after scoping. Timeline starts when you provide all content.

What content do I need to provide?

+

Business name, logo, page text (we can help write it), photos (we'll use free stock otherwise), contact details, and any design references. We guide you step by step.

Can I review the design before launch?

+

Yes. We share a live preview link after the initial design (2–3 days). You get up to 3 revision rounds before final launch.

Do you help with domain and hosting?

+

Yes — we guide you through purchasing a domain and setting up hosting. We recommend Hostinger or GoDaddy, then deploy the site and connect it to your domain.

Technical

Will it work on mobile?

+

100%. Every site is mobile-first and responsive — tested on phones, tablets, and desktops of all sizes.

Will I rank on Google?

+

We set up SEO on all plans. Pro includes full optimization. Ranking takes 1–3 months typically — we set you up correctly from day one.

Can I update content myself after launch?

+

For HTML sites we provide an editing guide for VS Code. Custom plan sites have an admin panel. During support period you can also WhatsApp us for changes.
